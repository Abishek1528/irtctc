SCREENSHOT: Issue 3 - Redundant Class Selection Dropdowns
========================================================

File: issue3-redundant-class-dropdowns-screenshot.jpg (to be added)

Description:
The IRCTC "BOOK TICKET" form on the homepage (irctc.co.in) showing:
- Top section (above):
  - From field with train icon
  - To field with location icon  
  - DD/MM/YYYY date field showing 28/05/2026
- Middle section (scrolled view):
  - "All Classes" dropdown (with seat/grid icon) - showing "All Classes" as selected
  - "GENERAL" dropdown (with grid/seat icon) - showing "GENERAL" as selected
- Checkbox options:
  - ☐ Person With Disability Concession
  - ☐ Flexible With Date
  - ☐ Railway Pass Concession
- Orange "Search Trains" button

Captured: May 28, 2026 at https://www.irctc.co.in/ (scrolled to show lower form)

Observations:
- Two separate class-related dropdowns present
- Both are visible simultaneously on the same form
- "All Classes" suggests a catch-all / unfiltered option
- "GENERAL" is ambiguous - could mean:
  a) General/3rd Class (an actual Indian Railways class)
  b) A generic/unspecified category
  c) General seating (non-specific)
- No clear label explaining the relationship between these two fields
- Users must guess which to select or whether to select both
- Unclear information architecture - redundant and confusing
- Violates principle of "one control per action"
